CleanShot

Support

CleanShot is a local-only screenshot styling app for Mac. This page is the official support entry point.

Quick help

  • Drag an image into the editor or use the import command.
  • Choose a frame, background, shadow, padding, and corner radius.
  • Preview the final composition before exporting.
  • Use App Store, Twitter, Instagram, or custom output sizes.
